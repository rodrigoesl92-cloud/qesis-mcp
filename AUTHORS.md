## Machine assistance, disclosed

Parts of the code, the documentation and the operational records in this
repository were produced with machine assistance, under the author's
specification and direction and subject to the gates in `CLAUDE.md` and
`sovereign-infra/ops/GOVERNANCE.md`.

**No automated system is an author of this work, and none holds or claims any
right in it.** An AI system cannot be listed as an author because it cannot take
responsibility for the work, which is the position of ICMJE, COPE and every
major publisher. The protectable subject matter claimed here is the
specification, the selection, the arrangement, the method and the data, all of
which are the author's.

## Machine attribution in the record

Which runtime produced which artefact is recorded per artefact, in commit
trailers, in `ops/LESSONS_LEDGER.md` and in the session reports under `ops/`.
That record exists so a defect can be traced to a process. Under Rule R-1 of
`CLAUDE.md` attribution is data, not blame, and it is not a claim of authorship
or of any right.

Commit trailers naming a runtime are a version-control convention for tracing a
change. They are not an assertion of co-authorship and are not to be read as one.
