Single source of truth for project status. Maintained by the assistant after every working session; decisions live in the Git decision log (`_GOVERNANCE\DECISION_LOG.md`) — this board mirrors status only.

## Ops tickets (D-028)

Every problem written into an operational log becomes a ticket below, owned by a named agent. A ticket closes only when the log entry is resolved on disk with evidence, and both the open and the close appear in that day's ops report. Status **Blocked** means it is waiting on Rico.

Standing as of 2026-07-28: **16 tickets, 5 closed, 5 blocked on Rico, 6 open with agents.**
