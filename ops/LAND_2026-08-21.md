## The one number worth reading before you commit

```
paired states      33 of 35        HKG and TWN absent, both already withheld under BIG
spearman rho       -0.4946   p 0.003437
pearson r          -0.5494   p 0.000927
verdict            CORROBORATED
```

The sign is the predicted one. ESE is a stress axis and ETI system performance is
a performance score, so convergent validity predicted a negative correlation and
the gate exits non-zero if it is not negative or if the magnitude falls under 0.30.

The magnitude is the informative half. Near -0.95 would have shown ESE to be a
restatement of an existing index and would have been bad news for the paper.
Near zero would have shown it measuring nothing recognisable. Moderate and
negative is convergent validity without redundancy, which is the result you want
and the one you can defend.

It corroborates **ESE**, which is a diagnostic axis excluded from the composite
under D-044. It does not corroborate the headline number, and the passage in
`ops/PRIOR_ART_ETI_2026.md` says so before a reviewer can.
